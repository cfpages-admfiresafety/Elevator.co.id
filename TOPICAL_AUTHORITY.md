# Topical Authority — elevator.co.id

Last reviewed: 2026-07-23 (Asia/Jakarta)

## Role and boundary

Hub pengetahuan Indonesia tentang elevator dan vertical-transportation systems: kebutuhan, tipe, traffic, bangunan, mekanisme, controls, keselamatan, instalasi, commissioning, operasi, maintenance, troubleshooting, modernization, procurement, dan lifecycle.

Primary audience: Building owner, homeowner, developer, architect, engineer, facility manager, procurement team, operator, maintenance provider, inspector, dan pengguna yang memerlukan keputusan elevator berbasis bukti.

Cannibalization controls apply only among URLs on **elevator.co.id**. Other owned domains are independent editorial properties and may cover the same query, entity, or point of view. Cross-domain overlap never suppresses a useful topic.

Neutral guides explain decisions. Existing commercial routes retain transactional intent, with /kontak-kami/ owning project-specific contact/quote intent. Indonesia is the primary geography. No local page may be produced by swapping place names; local substance requires verified differences in climate, regulation, supply, architecture, operations, service capability, data, or a real case.

## Existing coverage and risks

- Sedikitnya 910 URL tampak location/promotional-template based dan tidak membuktikan service presence atau substantive local answer.
- Sebanyak 939 REST/API URLs di sitemap dapat membuang crawl attention dan tidak merepresentasikan authority content.
- Passenger, building, office, residential, hospital, panoramic, car, cargo, and food-lift landing pages overlap strongly unless entity and use boundaries are explicit.
- Elevator is life-safety equipment; structure, electrical, controls, rescue, fire response, testing, and maintenance guidance require current primary rules and competent review.
- Absolute quality, warranty, standard, geographic coverage, or safety claims on legacy pages require evidence before reuse in neutral articles.

Every proposed article must pass the one-query, outline, SERP-intent, entity-attribute, and title/slug similarity tests against other proposed pages and all existing routes on this domain. Cross-domain matches are deliberately excluded from this collision test.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Use current Indonesian government, regulator, standards-body, accredited-lab, manufacturer technical, university/research, and recognized professional sources; record version/access dates.
- Do not name clauses, dimensions, ratings, tolerances, test loads, rescue steps, prices, warranty, certification, licensing, or service areas until verified from current primary evidence.
- Structural, facade, fire, glass, electrical, mechanical, controls, work-at-height, accessibility, emergency, and other safety-critical content requires a competent reviewer and explicit stop conditions.
- Separate observed repository/field evidence, sourced fact, calculation assumption, manufacturer claim, professional judgment, and editorial hypothesis.
- Calculators publish formula, units, assumptions, limits, worked examples, version history, and cases requiring professional design.
- Case studies require a real project, permission, dated inputs, constraints, process, outcome, limitations, and original or properly licensed evidence. Never fabricate firsthand experience.
- Use only original, licensed, or permission-backed visual assets with recorded provenance. AI illustration may explain a schematic concept but is never proof of a product, project, defect, or test.
- Price pages use dated cost models and drivers, not timeless promises. Commercial pages state scope, exclusions, proof, process, and warranty/service conditions honestly.
- Re-run same-domain route/title/H1/query collision checks before every draft. Do not flag other domains as cannibalization.
- Never mass-produce local pages; unique location evidence and a functioning service path are mandatory.
